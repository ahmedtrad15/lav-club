## 2. Goal & Target Audience

**Goal:** A web store that turns visitors into orders as fast as possible — simple, direct, no friction.

**Target audience:**
- People with **large or heavy loads** of laundry who **don't have time** to do it themselves.
- **Wedding dress businesses** (rental shops, bridal boutiques) — a priority segment.

## 4. Brand & Visual Style

- **Colors:** related to cleanliness and freshness — **blues and greens** (plus white for a clean feel).
- **First impression:** catchy and eye-catching from the very first screen (hero section).
- **Illustrations:** vector graphics only, no real photos —
  - big washing-machine vectors in the backgrounds,

- **Animations:** smooth, fun animations throughout the user experience (e.g. floating bubbles, spinning drum, add-to-cart effects, step transitions).

## 6. The Store (Product Selection)

**Principle:** easy and direct — **no categories**, everything on one screen.

- Each product is represented by an **icon** (no real pictures).
- Flow per product:
  1. User clicks an icon (e.g. 👖 trousers)
  2. Selects the **quantity**
  3. Clicks **Add to cart**
- Repeat for any other item.

**Pricing:**
- Each product has a **fixed washing price**.
- The total is **calculated and displayed automatically** as the user adds items.

**Special products (highlighted):**
- **Wedding dresses** (and other special items) get a dedicated, visually highlighted section — this targets bridal businesses.

**Sales boosters:**
- Discounts (promotions, volume discounts…)
- Upsells (suggested extras in the cart / checkout)

---

## 7. Ordering Flow

### 7.1 Pickup

- Pickup is always **the day after** the order is placed.
- The user chooses a time slot:
  - **10:00 – 12:00**
  - **16:00 – 19:00**
- *Not available at these times? Contact us.*

### 7.2 Delivery

The user picks their delivery date with a **date picker**.

| Option | Earliest delivery | Extra cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | 48 hours after pickup | Free |
| **"I'm in a rush" (Express)** | 24 hours after pickup | **+5 DT** |

The date picker must only allow valid dates depending on the selected option.

### 7.3 Summary of the customer journey

```
Home → Pick items (icons + quantity) → Cart (auto total, discounts, upsells)
     → Address (must be in service area) → Pickup slot (tomorrow, morning/evening)
     → Delivery option (Standard / Rush +5 DT) + date → Confirm order
```
